## Bio

I'm Francesco Pira, a software engineer and technical lead from Catania, Italy, working across cloud-native systems, applied AI, developer tooling, and open source.

I have spent more than a decade building software and, today at Leonardo, I lead an engineering team designing and building immersive simulators for specialized operators while staying hands-on with architecture, implementation, CI/CD, cloud integration, and technical delivery. I own customer-facing technical decisions, turning broad requirements into roadmaps and working systems.

Outside work, I build and publish open-source tools, with Rust my language of choice, and experiment with practical LLM engineering, agentic workflows, local inference, quantization, and hardware-aware AI systems to build my next venture.

I'm a curious mind by nature: I like understanding how things work, questioning the why, fixing what's broken, and turning ambiguous problems into working systems, which sometimes leads me to shape my own tools when the existing ones do not quite fit.
